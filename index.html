Diabetic retinopathy screening

Smart India Hackathon 2026, problem statement 26038

Give it a photo of the back of the eye and it answers clear, refer or escalate to a person. A ResNet-50 grades the image. A Grad-CAM heatmap of where the model looked is then checked against lesion evidence from two independent detectors, and a rule-based policy escalates whenever the image is poor or the evidence disagrees, instead of trusting the classifier alone.

At the frozen operating point it reaches sensitivity 0.982 and specificity 0.9172 on validation. Accuracy alone is never reported.

What didn't make it

One configuration let the pipeline decide more cases without a person, but it sent two referable patients home3 where the classifier alone sent none. It was rejected, and the reason is written down as a decision record.

MATLAB, Simulink, ResNet-50, U-Net. A research prototype, not a medical device.

Agent spend policy engine

Decides whether an AI agent may pay for something, before any money moves

Approved payments settle for real on Algorand testnet through the x402 protocol: request, get a 402, pay, retry, get a 200. The APIs behind the paywall are real too, with live weather from Open-Meteo and company lookups from SEC EDGAR.

Every decision, allowed or blocked, goes into a log where each entry carries the hash of the one before it, and the latest hash is written to Algorand. Anyone can confirm the record wasn't rewritten in about ten seconds4, without trusting whoever runs the system. The dashboard has a button that tampers with a past record, and verification names the exact entry that changed.

A new reader can go from git clone to a paid API call in about fifteen minutes5.

FastAPI, Pydantic, Algorand, x402, testnet USDC.

Ayur-Ai

Names an Ayurvedic medicinal leaf from a photo, then looks up what it's used for

A ResNet18 fine-tuned in PyTorch identifies 30 species, and a TF-IDF search over a herb knowledge base finds their uses or matches herbs to a symptom. It runs fully offline, with no LLM and no API keys, and trains in about two minutes on a laptop GPU.

Many photos in the dataset show the same physical leaf, so near-duplicates are grouped and kept on one side of every split. Under that rule it scores 98.64% accuracy6 in 5-fold cross-validation. A single split swings between 0.87 and 1.007 depending on the random seed, which is why the cross-validated number is the one quoted.

PyTorch, scikit-learn, Streamlit. Educational, not medical advice.

Liquid Glass 14 stars

Most Omarchy themes are a color palette. This one is a material. The bar, launcher, notifications and window borders are only white and black at low opacity, so they pick up their color from whatever is behind them. Sadie My Love

A copper and oxblood theme whose colors follow the wallpaper without turning into something else. Each wallpaper rotates the hue of one hand-tuned palette in Oklch, and the rotation is capped at 28 degrees9 so every text color keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast.
